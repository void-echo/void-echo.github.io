I am a Ph.D. student at [CASIA](http://www.ia.cas.cn/) ([NLPR](http://www.nlpr.ia.ac.cn/cn/index.html)), advised by Prof. [Qiang Liu](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?hl=en&user=D-lKLcMAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate). Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from [Shandong University](https://www.sdu.edu.cn/), and worked as a research intern at <a href="https://z.ai/" style="text-decoration: none;"><img src="{{ '/assets/img/zai-logo.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="Z.ai" style="height: 1.15em; width: 1.15em; vertical-align: -0.2em; margin-right: 0.18em;"></a>[Z.ai](https://z.ai/) and <a href="https://www.realai.ai/" style="text-decoration: none;"><img src="{{ '/assets/img/realai-logo.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="RealAI" style="height: 1.15em; width: 1.15em; vertical-align: -0.2em; margin-right: 0.18em;"></a>[RealAI](https://www.realai.ai/).

My research focuses on **AI for Science**, spanning **Genomics, RNA Biology, Molecular Representation, LLM Agents, and MLSys**. I develop generalizable, interpretable deep learning models, agentic systems, and GPU-accelerated algorithms to decode biological sequences and molecular structures—supporting both biological mechanism discovery and drug development. My work includes first- and co-first-author publications in *Nature Communications* and *Nucleic Acids Research*, and I received the National Scholarship for Graduate Students in 2026.

Chinese, English & Japanese speaker, ENFP. ✨ Chasing for Courage, Power and Freedom. Love Life.
